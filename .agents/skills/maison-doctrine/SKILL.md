# Maison doctrine

Learnt from studying how the great houses publish (`maison/references/`), applied only to the
user's own brand, a client's brand or an invented house — never to imitate a real house.

| # | Principle | Skill | Rubric criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Culture first, product second | `maison/doctrine/01-culture-first/SKILL.md` | one_idea, voice |
| 02 | The cover shows one singular object chosen for its beauty or cultural meaning, with extreme restraint and generous negative space | `maison/doctrine/02-singular-cover/SKILL.md` | restraint, one_idea |
| 03 | Drawing is a recurring, named, numbered series archived by year, month and series, on a neutral paper-light ground that lets the drawing carry all the weight | `maison/doctrine/03-drawing-series/SKILL.md` | craft, cohesion |
| 04 | Pair archive and old-master material (CC0 museum art) with contemporary work in the same spread, so each reads the other differently | `maison/doctrine/04-past-informs-present/SKILL.md` | archive_dialogue |
| 05 | Frame the issue as an incomplete inventory of a place or an idea, sequenced along a clear arc | `maison/doctrine/05-inventory-and-arc/SKILL.md` | one_idea, cohesion |
| 06 | Each issue gets a deliberately built, cohesive image set | `maison/doctrine/06-commissioned-image-set/SKILL.md` | cohesion, craft |
| 07 | Each issue may take a guest art director's voice (a style brief), while the masthead system, grid and typography stay constant | `maison/doctrine/07-guest-voice-one-identity/SKILL.md` | typography, cohesion |
| 08 | Biannual thinking, long-form features, multilingual editions with Arabic RTL as a first-class edition written natively, and production quality as identity | `maison/doctrine/08-collectible-slow-cadence/SKILL.md` | arabic_edition, craft |
| 09 | Restraint is the luxury | `maison/doctrine/09-restraint/SKILL.md` | restraint, typography |
| 10 | Copy is aspirational and precise | `maison/doctrine/10-precise-voice/SKILL.md` | voice |

Read all ten before a maison-issue, maison-drawing-series or maison-campaign stage. The critique
gate (`maison/critique/rubric.md`) scores against them; below 4 goes back to its stage.
