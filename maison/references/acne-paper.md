# Study note: a fashion house's culture magazine (Acne Paper)

Fetched 2026-11-20 from the magazine's about page.

## Facts
- Published by a fashion house, edited by one editor-in-chief. First run 2005–2014, biannual;
  relaunched 2021 redesigned, larger (around 500 pages), higher production values; a book
  edition in 2021.
- Fashion is **one element** in a cultural mix; fashion stories use clothes from many labels —
  the magazine is not a catalogue for its publisher.

## Theming
Each issue takes **one unifying theme** and explores it across disciplines: photography, art,
literature, theatre and dance, sculpture, street photography, meditation, queer culture,
15th-century tapestry beside contemporary work. "The past informs the present" is a stated lens.

## Voice and direction
Refined, curious, interdisciplinary; reverent toward history, forward in framing. Elegant,
design-led art direction; contributors across generations.

## Principles taken
One theme per issue (5); archive beside contemporary in the same issue (4); production values
and format as identity — a paper edition worth keeping (8); the house's own product kept out of
the centre (1).
