# Study note: an online house magazine's monthly "art guide" (Hermès, La Lanterne)

Fetched 2026-11-20 from the series index page (Japan-facing site, English navigation). The index
carries titles, thumbnails and dates, not article bodies, so this note covers structure and voice.

## What it is
A series inside the house's online magazine. Sibling series sit beside it: stories, a drawing
series, behind-the-scenes, the house's "secrets", and an about page.

## Structure
- An archive ("back numbers") organised **by year and month**; one entry per month.
- Each entry: one landscape image, a short title, a date line. Nothing else on the card.
- Every title is built on one verb, "Drawing ...", followed by a subject (a carriage, colour play,
  punk, functional beauty, culture and daily life). The series is a **named, recurring frame**
  that any subject can pass through.

## Voice
Short, gently conceptual titles. No call to action, no product, no price on the index. The
house appears only as the logo, the copyright line and a link home: its presence is
institutional, not commercial.

## Layout impressions
A quiet list archive, uppercase month labels, generous vertical space, the series name set as an
image banner, a mobile-first reading posture.

## Principles taken (not the look)
- A drawing series with a fixed verb and a numbered/dated archive (doctrine 3).
- Product absent from the editorial surface (doctrine 1).
- Restraint: one image and one line per entry (doctrine 2, 9).
