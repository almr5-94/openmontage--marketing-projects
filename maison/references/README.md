# Maison references: study notes only

Written analyses of how the great houses publish. **Text only.** No brand image, logo, masthead
or photograph is ever saved here or in `projects/`; reference pages may be viewed for analysis,
never downloaded, cropped or used as a generation reference. These notes teach principles; the
work Maison makes is for the user's own brand, a client who owns theirs, or an invented house.

`brand_lint` deliberately does not scan this folder: study notes may name what they study.
Production files may not.
