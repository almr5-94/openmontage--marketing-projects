# Study note: a house magazine art-directed by an agency (Le Monde d'Hermès, Lane & Associates)

Fetched 2026-11-20 from the agency's case page. The page describes scope and concept; it does not
show typography, grid, covers or paper, so none is inferred here.

## Facts
- Artistic direction of the house's **biannual** publication.
- **Two guest-art-directed issues**, one concept carried across both.
- **1,500+ commissioned images**, about 40 contributors (photographers, artists, illustrators).
- Theme: the house's historic building — its history, its character, its influence — framed as
  an **"incomplete inventory"** of the place.

## Principles taken
- An issue as an incomplete inventory with an arc: history (story) → character → influence (5).
- A commissioned, cohesive body of images per issue, not a pile of pictures (6).
- A guest voice per issue inside one constant house identity (7).
- A place, not a product, as the subject (1).
