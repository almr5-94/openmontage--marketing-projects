# 10. Precise voice

Copy is aspirational and precise: specific, sensory and craft-literate. Never salesy, never superlative-stuffed, never operational.

## Do

- Name materials, tools, gestures, places and dates. One evocative display line per page; facts belong in the body, operational facts only in the colophon.
- Banned in display copy: timelines, 'our team will', CTAs, discounts, urgency, filler (elevate, unlock, seamless, curated experience) - the AP-1 copy lint enforces it.
- Superlatives need evidence or go.

## Check (the critic asks)

- Underline every adjective. Is each one doing work a noun could not?

Scored in `maison/critique/rubric.md` under: voice.

Never name, quote the look of, or recreate a real house: describe qualities instead (`brand_lint` blocks names).
