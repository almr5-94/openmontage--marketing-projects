# 01. Culture first

Culture first, product second: the issue is a gift and a world to enter; the product appears only inside stories, through how it was made and why it mattered.

## Do

- Every story is about craft, art history, science, invention, a subculture, a place or the house's own heritage.
- The product may appear only inside a story (its making, its maker, the problem it solved), never as a standalone ad on an editorial page.
- Count product-led pages in the flatplan: at most one story in five may centre on a product, and it must be told through process or history.

## Check (the critic asks)

- Which page would still be worth reading if the product were removed from it? All but the product stories must be.
- Is there any page that reads as a catalogue or an ad? Move it out of the editorial run or rewrite it as a story.

Scored in `maison/critique/rubric.md` under: one_idea, voice.

Never name, quote the look of, or recreate a real house: describe qualities instead (`brand_lint` blocks names).
