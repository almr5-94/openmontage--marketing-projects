# 07. Guest voice one identity

Each issue may take a guest art director's voice (a style brief), while the masthead system, grid and typography stay constant.

## Do

- Record the guest voice as a short brief in the issue brief: palette shift, image temperament, pacing. It never changes the masthead, the grid or the type families.
- Keep the house identity in house.yaml; the issue overrides only what the guest brief names.

## Check (the critic asks)

- Put this issue's cover beside the previous one: same house at first glance, different voice at second?

Scored in `maison/critique/rubric.md` under: typography, cohesion.

Never name, quote the look of, or recreate a real house: describe qualities instead (`brand_lint` blocks names).
