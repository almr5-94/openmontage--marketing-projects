# 08. Collectible slow cadence

Biannual thinking, long-form features, multilingual editions with Arabic RTL as a first-class edition written natively, and production quality as identity.

## Do

- Paper stock, finishes and trim are design decisions recorded in house.yaml and proofed with the paper-stock simulation.
- Arabic is written natively in literary register, set RTL with Arabic display and text faces, Latin digits; never machine-translated in tone.
- Features are long-form (1,500+ words) with room to breathe.

## Check (the critic asks)

- Would a reader keep this on a shelf for ten years?
- Read the Arabic aloud: does it read as Arabic prose, or as English in Arabic letters?
