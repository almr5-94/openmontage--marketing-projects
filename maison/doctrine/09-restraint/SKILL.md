# 09. Restraint

Restraint is the luxury: few typefaces (one high-contrast serif and one quiet sans or mono), a disciplined grid, small-caps labels, bilingual headers where the house is bilingual, no visual shouting.

## Do

- At most two families per script, from print-studio/fonts/FONTS.yaml. Labels in small caps with open tracking; no bold shouting, no underlines, no coloured boxes.
- No cards, chips, badges, pills, icons, breadcrumbs or diagrams (AP-3, AP-7). Folios and section openers follow the magazine's typographic system.
- Negative space is a material: every page keeps a quiet field.

## Check (the critic asks)

- Count the elements on the page. Remove one. Is the page better? Repeat.

Never name, quote the look of, or recreate a real house: describe qualities instead (`brand_lint` blocks names).
