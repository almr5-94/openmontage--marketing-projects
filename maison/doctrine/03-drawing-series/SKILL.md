# 03. Drawing series

Drawing is a recurring, named, numbered series archived by year, month and series, on a neutral paper-light ground that lets the drawing carry all the weight.

## Do

- Name the series with a fixed frame ('Drawing <theme> No. <n>') and keep a fixed hand: medium look, line weight, palette, paper.
- Before plate 1, make a style-lock sheet; every later plate is compared with it.
- Archive pages group plates by year, month and series; the series index lists number, title, year.

## Check (the critic asks)

- Do plates No. 1 and No. 30 look like the same artist? Compare them on the style-lock sheet.
- Is the ground quiet enough that the drawing carries the page?

Scored in `maison/critique/rubric.md` under: craft, cohesion.

Never name, quote the look of, or recreate a real house: describe qualities instead (`brand_lint` blocks names).
