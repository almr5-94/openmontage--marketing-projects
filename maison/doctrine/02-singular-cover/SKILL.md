# 02. Singular cover

The cover shows one singular object chosen for its beauty or cultural meaning, with extreme restraint and generous negative space.

## Do

- One object: a hat, a chair, a garment, a drawing, a tool, a museum object. Never a pack shot, never a collage of many things.
- At least 40% of the cover is quiet ground. The masthead is constant; cover lines are few (two at most) and small.
- Sources, in order: a CC0 museum object, a plate from the house's drawing series, an elected Runway generation from a full micro-brief.

## Check (the critic asks)

- Can the cover be described in five words? If not, it holds more than one idea.
- Remove the masthead: does the object alone still stop the eye?

Scored in `maison/critique/rubric.md` under: restraint, one_idea.

Never name, quote the look of, or recreate a real house: describe qualities instead (`brand_lint` blocks names).
