# 05. Inventory and arc

Frame the issue as an incomplete inventory of a place or an idea, sequenced along a clear arc: its story, then its character, then its influence.

## Do

- Draft 20-40 candidate stories and objects (the local model or Claude drafts; the human or Claude elects).
- Sequence the elected list into three movements: story (where it comes from), character (what it is like), influence (what it changed).
- Record the inventory and the arc as a maison_inventory artifact; the contents page shows the three movements.

## Check (the critic asks)

- Read only the contents page: does it tell a story with a beginning, a middle and an end?

Scored in `maison/critique/rubric.md` under: one_idea, cohesion.

Never name, quote the look of, or recreate a real house: describe qualities instead (`brand_lint` blocks names).
