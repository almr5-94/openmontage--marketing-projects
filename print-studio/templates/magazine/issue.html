Feature

Every frame is a decision

A working week inside a four-person studio, where the brief is short, the budget is shorter and every choice shows on the page.

Words by the editors · Pictures by the studio
Feature3

Monday starts with a single page. Not a deck, not a mood board -- one page that says who the piece is for, what they should feel, and the one thing they must remember. Everything after that is measured against it.

The studio keeps the page pinned above the shared screen. When a picture is beautiful but says the wrong thing, the page wins. When a headline is clever but slows the reader down, the page wins again.

By Tuesday the team has a flatplan: a map of every page in the issue, drawn small enough to fit on one sheet. Each box carries a page number, a working title and a rough word count, so nobody writes 1,800 words for a slot that holds 900.

"When a picture is beautiful but says the wrong thing, the page wins."

Pictures come next. Some are shot, some are drawn, some are generated, and each one is checked against the page it lands on: does it leave room for the headline, does the subject face into the spread rather than off it, does it survive being printed at 230 by 300 millimetres.

Wednesday is for words. Copy is written to length, then cut to fit, then read aloud. Captions get the same care as the feature itself, because readers look at pictures first and captions second.

Thursday the layout is set, proofed and set again. Every text box is checked for overflow, every image for resolution, every number for the right digits. Friday the file goes to print, and the studio starts the next single page.

مقالة قصيرة

كل صورة قرار

ورشة صغيرة من 4 أشخاص تصنع عددًا كاملًا في 5 أيام.

يبدأ الأسبوع بصفحة واحدة تقول لمن نكتب، وماذا نريد أن يشعر القارئ، وما الشيء الوحيد الذي يجب أن يتذكره. كل ما يأتي بعد ذلك يُقاس عليها.

في اليوم الثاني يرسم الفريق خريطة العدد: كل صفحة في مربع صغير، عليه رقمها وعنوانها وعدد كلماتها التقريبي، حتى لا يكتب أحد 1800 كلمة لمساحة تتسع لـ 900 فقط.

ثم تأتي الصور، وتُفحص كل صورة على الصفحة التي ستُطبع فيها بمقاس 230 × 300 ملم.
