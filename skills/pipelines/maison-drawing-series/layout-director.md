# Layout Director — Drawing Series

- Plate pages: the drawing on a paper-light ground with generous margins, the title in the series frame,
  a small caption (medium look, year, number).
- A **series index** page: every plate small, numbered.
- An **archive page** grouping series by year, month and series.
Use `editorial_layout` (art_page with fit "plate", contents, text_page) and run its checks.
