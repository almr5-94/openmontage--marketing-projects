# Plates Director

Make each plate in the elected hand at print resolution, titled by the series frame.
Keep a seed per plate so any plate can be re-made exactly. Archive references (CC0 museum drawings)
may guide composition but are credited in provenance. Every image that may show a person passes `run_face_gate` (photo or cartoon mode) before it is placed; a failure moves the file to `components/` with the reason, never deletes it.
Write `asset_manifest`.
