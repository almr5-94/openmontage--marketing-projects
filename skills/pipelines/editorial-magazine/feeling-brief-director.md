# Feeling Brief Director — Editorial Magazine

## Job
Turn the request into a `print_brief` whose centre is **one feeling** (e.g. "patience", "salt
light", "the hour before a storm"). Everything later is derived from it.

## Ask (one short round, then propose defaults)
- The feeling and the theme it serves; who reads it and why they keep it.
- Page count (a multiple of 4), trim: `a4`, `us_letter` or `magazine_230x300`; 3 mm bleed.
- Languages: EN, AR, or both. Arabic is written natively, never machine-translated in tone.
- Mandatory content (names, facts, the user's own pictures).

## Write
`print_brief` with `kind: magazine`, the feeling in `tone`, formats with mm sizes, languages,
success criteria the review can check ("every spread carries one real image", "no diagrams").
Log the feeling and format as a `concept_selection` decision.
