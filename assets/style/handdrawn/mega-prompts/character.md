# Mega-prompt 1 — the character action plate

Fill every bracket with real content, then keep **one** family block and delete the other two.
Numbers are art-direction targets for review, not constraints a text prompt can enforce.

```text
[FRAMING: full-body plate | medium action plate], [ASPECT RATIO], one [SUBJECT] performing
[CLEAR VISIBLE ACTION] with [ONE NECESSARY PROP OR NONE], placed [FRAME POSITION], with
[GAZE OR ORIENTATION] and a readable [DOMINANT GESTURE]. Preserve [CRITICAL HAND-TO-PROP OR
FOOT-TO-SUPPORT CONTACT]. Leave [QUIET-SPACE TARGET] around the action and keep the decisive
silhouette gap open.
```

## Family A — paper folk

```text
hdx_paperfolk. Compact adult of about four cranial-face units, elongated rounded face, pale
circular eye enclosures with small directed pupils, compact mouth mark, minimal frontal nasal
indication, rounded trapezoid garment torso, narrow limbs with simplified joint bends,
pose-specific small hands, understated geometric footwear. Enclosed fine contours with
restrained width variation, sparse structural interior marks, differentiated local-color
clothing, quiet mottled paper field, calm facial fills, more material variation in hair and
garments than in skin. No volumetric body shading. Palette roles: [PAPER / GARMENTS / SKIN],
character features: [HAIR, GARMENT, AGE MARKERS].
```

Review targets: isolated plate keeps 70-90% quiet field; a lineup 35-55%; figure height
45-60% of the art height.

## Family B — incised silhouette

```text
hdx_incise. Archetype: [COMPACT 2.8-3.6 UNITS | ELONGATED 4.0-5.0 UNITS | CRESTED WARRIOR].
Build an opaque black silhouette with a designed projecting profile, narrow band-like limbs,
pointed feet and one dominant action direction. Articulate it with selected high-value incised
eye, hair, garment and hand marks. Keep the broad black interior clean of texture. Setting:
[SIMPLE ENVIRONMENT | ISOLATED COLOURED FIELD] with high contrast around head, action hand and
prop. No flesh-coloured modelling, no pale circular sclera.
```

Review targets: the silhouette alone must carry the action; white marks clarify, they do not
rescue a weak shape.

## Family C — bio texture

```text
hdx_biotext. Subject: [ORGANISM | HUMAN CLOSE-UP]. For an organism, preserve its attachment
logic - cap to stalk, branch to stem, body to appendage - with organic filled shapes and
selective structural marks. For a human close-up, keep enlarged irises with radial detail and
a tapered nasal plane with visible nostril marks. Palette: [MODE-SPECIFIC], detail concentrated
at [FOCAL STRUCTURE].
```

Evidence boundary: a full human body is not established for this family. A full-body C figure
is a new design decision, and must be labelled as one.

```text
Render a complete static illustration plate. No video player controls, progress bar, browser
chrome, seek preview, cursor, platform watermark or accidental interface text. Text, if needed,
belongs in [RESERVED LABEL AREA] and is verified separately.
```
