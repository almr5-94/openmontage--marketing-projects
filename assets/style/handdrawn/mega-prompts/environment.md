# Mega-prompt 2 — the establishing environment plate

```text
Wide establishing illustration, [ASPECT RATIO], [SETTING], organised around [PRIMARY OPENING OR
FOCAL LANDMARK]. Place [FOREGROUND MASS] at [POSITION], [MIDGROUND SUBJECT OR PATH] at
[POSITION], and [DISTANT ELEMENT] beyond it. Maintain [LOW-DETAIL OPENING TARGET] and a clear
route from [ENTRY AREA] to [FOCAL AREA]. Use [GROUND-CONTACT LOGIC] and [PERSPECTIVE MODE]
without adding a camera effect the style does not have.
```

## Family A — conceptual stage

```text
hdx_paperfolk, enclosed fine contour drawing, restrained contour-width variation, quiet mottled
paper field, shallow overlapping paper arrangement. A small set of enclosed outlined props with
deliberate empty margins: [ISOLATED PROP GROUP | SYMBOLIC STAIRS | PODIUMS | PINBOARD]. Keep
local contact exact - a shoe on a step, a pin through a card - while the global space stays
symbolic. Differentiate objects by local fill and contour, not by lighting.
```

## Family B — atmospheric setting

```text
hdx_incise, foreground-framed atmospheric landscape, localized environmental stippling,
soft-edged atmospheric surface fields, depth-separated environmental values. Near material
crisp and granular; distant boundaries softened toward [ATMOSPHERIC COLOUR]. Scene arrangement:
[WARM/COOL CHOICE]. Architecture from repeated designed forms and broad masses. If
[CENTRAL MONUMENT MODE], organise convergence and broad illumination around the object.
Figures, if any, keep their opaque silhouette treatment separate from the scenery.
```

Review target: a landscape keeps a 25-45% low-detail opening and three separated depth planes.

## Family C — botanical or biological setting

```text
hdx_biotext, organic filled-shape construction, selective structural drawing, object-specific
internal patterning, controlled surface irregularity. Layered plant or biological masses with
coherent branch and stem attachments, lobed vegetation, selective veins and growth rings.
Frame [OPEN WATER | PATH | SOIL WINDOW | OTHER OPENING] with unequal foreground masses.
Blue-green, olive and earth relationships plus [ONE SEMANTIC WARM ACCENT IF NEEDED]. Distant
elements simpler; keep the middle-distance opening readable.
```
